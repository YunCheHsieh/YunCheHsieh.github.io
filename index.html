Selected Work

Research, education, and entrepreneurship are different settings for the same problem-solving process.

01

GreenMind

A tracked rover for guava-orchard inspections, combining close-range imagery and sensor data into clear, practical field records.

  • Smart Agriculture
  • IoT
  • Venture Validation
GreenMind Website

The service combines field data-acquisition equipment, the GreenMind Console, and orchard data cards and inspection reports delivered to farmers. It turns scattered photographs and field observations into traceable inspection records.

02

Vortex Tube Measurement System

A multilevel learning experience using thermometers, thermocouples, and a vortex tube to connect macroscopic temperature with molecular kinetic energy.

  • Experimental Physics
  • ESP32
  • Data Systems

The study compared how inlet pressure, hot-end outlet width, and tube length affect cold-end temperature, then used student pre- and post-tests to assess learning outcomes. It received an Outstanding Poster Award at the 2026 Taiwan Physics Annual Meeting.

03

Science Education in Practice

Turning robots, 3D printing, and physics concepts into hands-on lessons that students can understand and explore.

  • Science Education
  • Hands-on Learning
  • Knowledge Translation

The work spans elementary, junior high, and senior high schools, teacher workshops, and public science events. Topics include line-following robots, aerodynamics, simple harmonic motion, waves, and rotation.
